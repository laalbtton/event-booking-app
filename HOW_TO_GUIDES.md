## 1. Getting Started

### Create an Account

1. Open Laal Button and tap **Sign Up**.
2. Enter your **Full Name**, **Email**, and a **Password** (minimum 6 characters).
3. Tap **Sign Up with Email**.
4. You will see a "Check Your Email!" screen — open your inbox and click the verification link.
5. Once verified, return to the app and log in.

> **Tip:** You can also sign up instantly with your Google account — tap **Continue with Google** and no email verification is required.

### Log In

1. Open the app and tap **Login**.
2. Enter your **Email** and **Password**.
3. Tap **Login with Email**.
4. You will be redirected to your Dashboard.

> If you see a message saying "Please verify your email address before logging in," check your inbox for the verification email first.

### Log In with Google

1. On the Login or Sign Up page, tap **Continue with Google**.
2. Choose your Google account.
3. You will be logged in automatically and redirected to the Dashboard.

## 2. Install Laal Button as an App

Laal Button can be installed on your device for a full-screen, app-like experience — no app store download needed.

### iPhone / iPad (iOS)

1. Open **Safari** and go to the Laal Button website.
2. Tap the **Share** button (the square with an arrow pointing up) at the bottom of the screen.
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**.
4. Confirm the name ("Laal Button") and tap **Add**.
5. The Laal Button icon will appear on your Home Screen — tap it to open the app in full-screen mode.

> **Important:** This only works in Safari. Chrome and other browsers on iOS do not support Add to Home Screen.

### Android

1. Open **Chrome** and go to the Laal Button website.
2. You may see an **"Install app"** banner at the bottom — tap **Install**.
3. If no banner appears: tap the **three-dot menu** (⋮) in the top-right corner and select **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**.
4. Confirm by tapping **Install**.
5. The app icon will appear in your app drawer and Home Screen.

### Desktop (Chrome)

1. Open Chrome and go to the Laal Button website.
2. Look for the **Install** icon (a monitor with a down arrow) in the address bar on the right side.
3. Click it and then click **Install** in the popup.
4. The app will open in its own window and a shortcut will be added to your desktop.

### Desktop (Edge)

1. Open Edge and go to the Laal Button website.
2. Click the **three-dot menu** (⋯) in the top-right corner.
3. Select **Apps** → **Install this site as an app**.
4. Confirm the name and click **Install**.

### Desktop (Safari on Mac)

1. Open Safari and go to the Laal Button website.
2. Click **File** in the menu bar → **Add to Dock**.
3. Confirm the name and click **Add**.

## 3. Set Up Your Profile

### Edit Your Profile

1. Tap **Profile** in the bottom navigation bar.
2. Tap the **pencil icon** (✏️) next to your name.
3. Update any of the following:
   - **Full Name**
   - **Bio** — a short description about yourself
   - **Website Link**
   - **Instagram Link**
   - **YouTube Link**
   - **Twitter Link**
4. Tap **Save** to apply your changes.

Your profile picture is automatically pulled from your Google account if you signed up with Google. It shows your initials if no picture is available.

### Share Your Profile

1. Go to your **Profile** page.
2. Tap the **Share** button (↗️) next to your name.
3. Your public profile link will be copied to your clipboard — paste it anywhere to share.

## 4. Buying Credits

### How Credits Work

- **1 credit = $1 CAD**
- Credits are used to book spots at events (most events cost 5–6 credits).
- Credits **never expire**.
- When you cancel a booking within the refund window, your credits are returned.

### Buy a Credit Pack

1. Tap **Buy Credits** on your Dashboard (or navigate to the Buy Credits page).
2. Choose from one of the available packs:
   - **5 credits** — $5 CAD
   - **10 credits** — $10 CAD *(Popular)*
   - **50 credits** — $50 CAD
3. Tap the **Buy** button on the pack you want.
4. You will be redirected to a secure **Stripe checkout** page.
5. Enter your payment details and complete the purchase.
6. Your credits will be added to your account immediately.

### Buy a Custom Amount

1. Go to the **Buy Credits** page.
2. Scroll to the **Custom amount** section.
3. Enter the number of credits you want (between 5 and 500).
4. Tap **Buy X credits**.
5. Complete payment on the Stripe checkout page.

### View Your Credit History

1. From the Dashboard, tap **Credits History** (next to "Buy Credits").
2. You will see:
   - Your **current credit balance** at the top.
   - A **full transaction history** showing every credit change — purchases, bookings, refunds, and admin adjustments.
   - Each entry shows the amount (+/-), type, and date.

## 5. Booking an Event

### Browse Available Events

1. Go to your **Dashboard**.
2. Scroll down to the **Available Events** section.
3. Use the tabs to filter:
   - **Perform** — Open mic events where you can sign up to perform.
   - **Attend** — Booked shows and ticketed events to attend as audience.
4. Each event card shows the title, date, location, credits required, and available spots.

### Book an Event

1. Find an event you want to attend on the Dashboard or tap into the Event Details page.
2. Tap **Book Event**.
3. The credits will be deducted from your balance.
4. You will see a confirmation message: "Event booked successfully!"
5. A **confirmation email** will also be sent to your inbox.
6. The event will appear under **My Bookings** on your Dashboard.

> **Note:** If an event requires more credits than you have, the button will show "Not enough credits." Go to Buy Credits first.

### Join the Waitlist

If an event is full:

1. The button will show **Join Waitlist** instead of "Book Event."
2. Tap **Join Waitlist** — your credits will be held.
3. If someone cancels, you will be automatically promoted to a confirmed spot.
4. You will receive a **notification and email** when you are promoted.
5. Your waitlist position is shown on the booking card.

### Set a Registration Alert

Some events have delayed registration (they open at a specific date/time):

1. On an event that hasn't opened registration yet, tap **Alert Me**.
2. You will receive an **email notification** when registration opens.
3. Come back and book your spot when it opens.

## 6. Managing Your Bookings

### View Your Bookings

1. Go to your **Dashboard**.
2. Under **My Activity**, the **My Bookings** tab shows all your current bookings.
3. Each booking shows:
   - Event name and date
   - Your booking status (Confirmed or Waitlist)
   - Refund eligibility info

### Cancel a Booking

1. Go to **My Bookings** on the Dashboard.
2. Find the booking you want to cancel.
3. Tap **Cancel Booking** (or **Leave Waitlist** if you are on the waitlist).
4. A confirmation dialog will appear showing:
   - How many credits will be refunded
   - Whether you qualify for a full refund based on the cancellation window
5. Confirm the cancellation.
6. Credits will be returned to your account (if eligible).

### Understanding Refunds

| Situation | Refund |
|---|---|
| Cancelled **before** the cancellation window (usually 4+ hours before the event) | **Full refund** — all credits returned |
| Cancelled **within** the cancellation window (less than 4 hours before) | **No refund** |
| Leaving the **waitlist** | **Full refund** — always |
| **Booked show** (invite-only event) | No refund (these are free, 0 credits) |
| Event with **food coupon** — coupon already redeemed | Coupon portion **not refunded** |
| Event with **food coupon** — coupon not redeemed | Full refund including coupon portion |

> The cancellation window is set per event (default is 4 hours). Check the event details to see the exact policy.

## 7. Event Details & Sharing

### View Event Details

1. Tap on any event card from the Dashboard.
2. The Event Details page shows:
   - **Title and description**
   - **Date and time**
   - **Venue** (tap for address, parking info, and accessibility)
   - **Credits required**
   - **Capacity** — how many spots are filled and how many remain
   - **Cancellation policy** — how many hours before the event you can cancel for a full refund
   - **Host** name (with link to their profile)
   - **Rating** (e.g., 18+, All Ages)
   - **Confirmed Attendees** list
   - **Waitlist** (if applicable)
   - **Event poster** (if uploaded)

### Share an Event

1. On the Event Details page, tap the **Share** button.
2. The event's **public link** is copied to your clipboard.
3. Anyone with this link can view the event without logging in — they will see event details, the attendee list, and prompts to sign up or log in to book.

## 8. Food Coupon Vouchers

### What Are Food Coupons?

Some events include a **food coupon** as part of the booking. When you book one of these events, part of your credits go towards a venue food coupon that you can redeem at the venue for food and drinks.

### View Your Coupons

1. Go to your **Dashboard**.
2. Under **My Activity**, tap the **My Coupons** tab.
3. You will see your coupons organized into:
   - **Active** — issued and ready to use
   - **Redeemed** — already used at the venue
   - **Expired / Cancelled** — no longer valid
4. Each coupon shows:
   - The event name and date
   - **Coupon code** (e.g., LB-XXXXXXXX) — tap to copy
   - **Value** in dollars
   - **Expiry date**
   - A **QR code** for easy scanning at the venue

### Redeem a Coupon at the Venue

1. When you arrive at the venue, show your **coupon QR code** or give the **coupon code** to the staff.
2. The venue staff will scan or enter your code.
3. The coupon value will be applied as a discount to your food/drink order.
4. Your coupon status will update to "Redeemed."

> **Important:** Coupons expire after a set time (check the expiry date on your coupon). If you cancel your booking after the coupon has been redeemed, the coupon portion of your credits will not be refunded.

## 9. Notifications

Laal Button keeps you informed with real-time notifications.

**How to view notifications:**
1. Tap the **Notifications** bell icon in the bottom navigation bar.
2. A red badge shows how many unread notifications you have.

**Types of notifications you may receive:**
- **Booking confirmed** — your spot at an event is confirmed.
- **Promoted to Confirmed** — you have been moved from the waitlist to a confirmed spot.
- **Waitlist position changed** — your position in the waitlist has moved.
- **Event cancelled** — an event you were booked for has been cancelled (with refund info).
- **Event updated** — an event you are booked for has changed details.
- **Event reminder** — a reminder 24 hours before an event you are attending.
- **New event poster available** — a poster has been published for an event.
- **Poster auto-post succeeded** — your event poster was auto-posted to Instagram.
- **Instagram connected** — your Instagram account is now linked for auto-posting.

**Managing notifications:**
- Notifications are **automatically marked as read** when you open the Notifications page.
- **Swipe left** on a notification to delete it.
- **Tap** a notification to navigate to the related event.

## 10. Creating & Managing Events (Event Creators)

> This section is for users with the **Event Creator** or **Admin** role. See [Apply to Become an Event Creator](#apply-to-become-an-event-creator) below if you don't have this role yet.

### Apply to Become an Event Creator

1. On your Dashboard, look for the blue **"Apply for Event Creator"** card.
2. Tap **Apply Now**.
3. Optionally write a message explaining why you would like to create events.
4. Tap **Submit Application**.
5. An admin will review your request. You will see one of these statuses:
   - **Pending** — your application is under review.
   - **Approved** — you can now create events! Go to **Manage Events** in the navigation.
   - **Rejected** — the admin may include a note. You can reapply.

### Create a New Event

1. Tap **Manage Events** in the bottom navigation bar.
2. Tap **+ Create Event**.
3. Fill in the event details:

| Field | Required | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Event Title | Yes | Name of your event |
| Description | Yes | Details about the event |
| Event Type | Yes | **Open Mic** (anyone can book) or **Booked Show** (invite-only) |
| Date & Time | Yes | When the event starts |
| Duration | No | Length of the event (0.5–6 hours) — auto-calculates end time |
| Venue | Yes | Select from the venue list |
| Credits Required | Yes* | How many credits to attend (*auto-set to 0 for booked shows) |
| Cancel Hours | Yes* | How many hours before the event users can cancel for a refund (*auto-set to 0 for booked shows) |
| Max Attendees | No | Maximum capacity — leave blank for unlimited |
| Rating | No | Age rating (18+, All Ages, 16+, 13+) |
| Theme | No | Event theme or category |
| Registration Opens At | No | Delay registration until a specific date/time |

4. **Optional — Add Tickets:** Check "Add tickets" to enable ticket sales. You can set it as an external event with a ticket URL, or set a price and quantity.
5. **Optional — Food Coupon:** For open mic events, check "Enable venue food coupon" to include a food coupon with each booking.
6. Tap **Create Event**.

### Manage Your Events

1. Go to **Manage Events** in the navigation.
2. You will see two tabs:
   - **Upcoming Events** — events that haven't happened yet.
   - **Past Events** — completed events.
3. For each event, you can:
   - **Edit Details** — update event info (upcoming events only).
   - **Cancel Event** — cancel the event and refund all attendees.
   - **Copy Public Link** — copy the shareable event URL.
   - **Upload/Update Poster** — add an event poster image.
   - **Generate QR Code** — create a QR code linking to the public event page.
   - **Manage Attendance** — track who attended.

### Upload an Event Poster

1. Go to your event in **Manage Events**.
2. Tap **Add Poster** (or **Update Poster** if one already exists).
3. Select an image file (PNG, JPEG, or WebP — max 10 MB).
4. Optionally add a **caption**.
5. Tap **Upload**.
6. Once uploaded, you can:
   - **Download** the poster image.
   - **Copy the poster link** to share.
   - **Share** via your device's share sheet.
   - **Remove** the poster.

> If you have connected your Instagram account, the poster can be auto-posted to your Instagram feed.

### Manage Attendance

1. Go to your event and tap **Manage Attendance** (or navigate to the attendance page).
2. You will see:
   - **Confirmed Bookings** — list of all confirmed attendees.
   - **Waitlist** — users waiting for a spot.
   - **Stats** — total registered, attended, no-show, pending.
3. For each attendee, toggle the switch to mark them as **Attended** or **No Show**.
4. You can **reorder** attendees by dragging them.
5. If food coupons are enabled, you can **scan or enter coupon codes** to redeem them.

### Invite Performers (Booked Shows)

For invite-only booked shows:

1. Go to the event's **Manage Attendance** page.
2. In the **Invites** section:
   - **Search for users** by name and send individual invites.
   - **Create an invite link** to share — set the max number of uses and expiry date.
   - Copy the invite link and share it with performers.
3. Track invite status: Pending, Accepted, or Declined.

### Generate a QR Code

1. Go to your event in **Manage Events**.
2. Tap **Generate QR Code**.
3. A QR code is displayed that links to the event's public page.
4. You can **copy the URL** or **view the public page** directly.
5. Display the QR code at your venue for attendees to scan.

## 11. Responding to Invites

If you have been invited to a booked show:

1. You will receive a notification and/or an invite link.
2. If you received a **link**, tap it and you will be taken to the app. The invite is automatically accepted.
3. If you received an **in-app invite**:
   - Go to your **Profile** page.
   - Tap the **Invites** tab.
   - You will see pending invites with event details.
   - Tap **Accept** or **Decline**.
4. Accepted invites will add you to the event as a confirmed attendee (no credits are charged for booked shows).
