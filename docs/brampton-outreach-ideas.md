# Brampton outreach ideas

Ideas for filling Wednesday open mics and weekend variety shows (English / Punjabi / Hindi) with as little ongoing work as possible. You already have a weekly digest, 48-hour reminders, Insider emails, and poster autopost for attendees. What’s missing is outreach to people who are **not already on the list**, aimed at Brampton, and language-matched for the weekend shows.

These can run on Inngest once built.

---

## 1. Brampton “fill the room” pings (best fit)

A job that looks 4–6 days ahead at Ryan’s Chai events. If a Wednesday mic or weekend show is under a spot/ticket threshold, it emails and/or pushes people who are **not booked**: GTA Desi Comedy members, past Ryan’s Chai attendees, and Insider members whose survey matches the language (Punjabi / Hindi / English). Skip anyone already confirmed.

**Overhead after launch:** none. You only tune the threshold if a room type feels too spammy.

**Status:** not built.

---

## 2. Venue Instagram autopost

You already queue posters for attendees. Add a second, public-facing post (or story) to the venue / Brampton comedy Instagram when a show is posted or when a room is still thin mid-week. Same image pipeline, different caption, no extra design work.

**Overhead after launch:** none once the account + caption templates are set.

**Status:** not built.

---

## 3. Bring-a-friend after booking

Right after someone books (or gets a ticket) for a Brampton show, send one extra email/SMS: “Bring someone who hasn’t been yet — here’s a share link.” Use an existing invite/share URL so you are not inventing a new product.

**Overhead after launch:** none. One extra send on the booking path.

**Status:** not built.

---

## 4. Always-current “This week in Brampton” page + calendar subscribe

A public page that always lists **this week’s** open mics and variety shows. One QR code on a flyer / at the venue / on Instagram that never goes stale. People can also **subscribe the calendar** (ICS / webcal) so new weeks land in Apple/Google Calendar without visiting the app.

**Overhead after launch:** none. The page is just the live event list, filtered to Brampton.

**Status:** implemented at `/brampton` with ICS subscribe (`/api/brampton/calendar`) and email updates signup.
